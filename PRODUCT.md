## Users

Primary users are clinical data managers, clinical operations leads, biostatisticians, safety teams, and trial sponsors at pharmaceutical, biotechnology, and contract research organizations. They work inside regulated study workflows where a single field must remain traceable from form design through analysis and reporting. Secondary audiences are prospective service clients and clinical technology professionals considering working with SevenHills.

## Product Purpose

SevenHills is building one end-to-end clinical software platform that connects CRF design, the Trial Master File, data capture and interlinking, clinical data management, pharmacovigilance, biostatistics, SAS analysis, and final report generation. The company also offers services across the same eight areas. Success means trial teams can follow one data thread from design to report instead of reconciling disconnected tools and manual handoffs.

## Positioning

The platform is organized around a single traceable data thread. A field is defined once in CRF design, captured once in EDC, and carried forward with its context into data management, safety review, analysis, and reporting. The company is early stage and pre-revenue; the product is in development.

## Operating Context

The company is incorporated in Ontario, Canada on June 13, 2024, and based in Scarborough, Toronto. Work is grounded in clinical trial documentation, edit checks, queries, coding dictionaries such as MedDRA and WHODrug, analysis datasets, SAS outputs, and study reports. Validation, audit trails, and traceability are first-order concerns, not later additions.

## Product Principles

1. One thread over many tools: every stage should read from and write to the same traceable record.
2. Enter once, carry forward: avoid duplicate entry and manual reconciliation.
3. Validation is designed in: audit trails, controlled definitions, and traceability belong in the architecture.
4. Product and services reinforce each other: service work informs the platform, and the platform improves delivery.
5. Honest early-stage language: say building and in development, never imply adoption or proof that does not exist.
