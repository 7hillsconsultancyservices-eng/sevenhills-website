# Home

## Scope and mode

Persuade. The public marketing site for SevenHills, a pre-revenue clinical data technology company. The home page introduces one end-to-end platform and routes visitors to the platform, services, about, careers, and contact pages.

## Audience and job

Clinical data managers, clinical operations leads, biostatisticians, safety teams, and trial sponsors evaluating whether SevenHills understands the full clinical data lifecycle. They need to see, quickly and credibly, how one connected flow differs from the disconnected tools and manual handoffs they live with today.

## Action and proof

Primary action: Explore the Platform. Secondary: Contact Us. Proof available: the eight-stage flow, the Ontario incorporation and Scarborough location, the product-plus-services model, and honest in-development language. No clients, testimonials, certifications, or adoption claims exist.

## Constraints

Canadian English. No em dashes. No invented proof. No stock photography. No phone number. Legal name appears only in the footer and on the Contact page legal line. Product described as building or in development. Responsive, keyboard accessible, WCAG 2.2 AA, static, fast.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a clinical data thread, not a SaaS feature grid. It refuses the category's stacked hero, icon-card rows, and dashboard screenshot by making one synthetic lab field visibly travel through all eight stages.

OWN-WORLD: White and pale grey paper, dark slate ink, teal #0F766E as the thread and #14B8A6 for small signals, hairline rules, tabular numerals, compact data labels, Archivo display with Inter text. No photography, gradients, glass, or decorative shadows.

STORY: The visitor understands that one CRF field can stay traceable from design to final report, believes SevenHills is building that connected platform, and explores the platform or contacts the company.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 12-column grid under the header. Left, roughly 55 percent: a three-line display headline, one sentence on the platform, two buttons, and a short honest stage line. Right, roughly 45 percent: a paper-like trial thread panel with a synthetic HGB field at the top and eight compact stage rows joined by a vertical teal line. No eyebrow label above the headline.

FORM: pinned brief, seed: none (pinned) "dark slate ... teal ... white and very light grey backgrounds, clean clinical / enterprise SaaS look".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
